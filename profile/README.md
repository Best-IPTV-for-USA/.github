**Q: Do I need a smart TV for IPTV?**

No. A regular TV with a $30–$50 Firestick, Roku stick or Android TV box works perfectly with any IPTV service.

**Q: Can I get a free trial first?**

Most providers advertise free trials (24–48 hours). Use them — test during evening peak hours on your own connection. If a provider won't let you test, that tells you something.

**Q: Can IPTV replace cable completely?**

For most people, largely yes — once you've confirmed the specific channels, sports networks and events you need are included.

**Q: Is IPTV legal?**

IPTV is a technology for delivering television over the internet. Its legality depends on the provider, the rights to distribute the content, and applicable laws. Don't assume every channel a service advertises is licensed for distribution — review the provider's terms, billing and refund policy before subscribing.
